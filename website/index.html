Download for Mac

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Open the disk image

    Double-click DummyPet.dmg in your Downloads folder.

  2. Drag it to Applications

    Drop the DummyPet icon onto the Applications folder in the window.

  3. Say hello

    Open DummyPet. Your dog runs in from the edge of the screen, and a dog icon appears in the menu bar.

Seeing a security warning?

DummyPet isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks before the first launch. Right-click the app and choose Open. On macOS 15 or later, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Or run this in Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DummyPet.app

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account. Just a dog.

Will it slow down my Mac?

It's a small native app (under 2 MB) that only redraws the area around the dog, so it barely touches your CPU. Clicks pass straight through to the windows behind it unless you're touching the dog or its toys.

Does it collect any data?

No. DummyPet never connects to the internet. Your dog's name and stats are saved on your Mac and nowhere else.

What happens when I quit the app?

Your dog waits for you. Its stats drift down slowly while you're away, but gently: it can't get sick or run away. Expect an enthusiastic welcome back.

Can I hide it for a meeting?

Click the dog icon in the menu bar and choose Hide. Choose Show when the meeting's over.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit DummyPet from its menu, then drag it from Applications to the Trash.
