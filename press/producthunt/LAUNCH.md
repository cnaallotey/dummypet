## Maker comment (post right after launch)

> Hey Product Hunt! 👋
>
> I built DummyPet because my desk felt empty and my landlord said no to a real dog.
>
> It's a little cartoon dog that lives along the bottom of your Mac screen. It wanders around, naps when it's tired, digs up "treasure" (mostly socks), and tells you when it's hungry or bored. You can:
>
> 🍖 feed it and listen to it crunch
> 🎾 flick the ball across your screen for fetch
> 🦮 take it for a walk where your cursor holds the leash
> 🛁 give it a bath, then dodge the shake
> 🎪 unlock 8 tricks as your bond grows (the backflip is worth it)
>
> Clicks pass straight through to your other windows unless you're touching the dog, so it never gets in the way. Everything is drawn in code, the barks are synthesized, it's under 2 MB, and it never connects to the internet.
>
> It's free. The site has a playable version of the dog if you want to say hi before downloading.
>
> One heads up: it isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first launch shows a security prompt. The download page explains the one-click way past it.
>
> I'd love to hear what tricks or features you'd want next. 🐶
